## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, sponsor-ready aesthetic for the Advanced Software Engineering (ASE) department at NMIT Bengaluru. It merges two distinct visual paradigms:
1. **AgentAI's Cinematic Dark Future:** Deep, obsidian surfaces, high-dynamic-range crimson illumination, focused radial vignettes, and rich glassmorphic overlays.
2. **Omni-V's Precision Industrial Engineering:** Hairline metallic borders, technical HUD micro-annotations, compartmentalized grid cells, and structured editorial layout.

### Brand Personality & Audience
- **Target Audience:** Engineering talent, elite academic researchers, enterprise corporate partners, and global tech sponsors looking for world-class technical execution.
- **Brand Attributes:** High-velocity, uncompromisingly rigorous, futuristic, premium, and mechanically precise.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Authority, technical competence, and the excitement of next-generation physical computing and AI development.

### Design Principles
- **Luminous Containment:** Bright crimson (#FF263B) and deep vermilion accents operate strictly as focused energy sources—glowing indicators, active states, key data readouts, and focal gradients. They never overwhelm the deep charcoal canvas.
- **Instrument-Grade Precision:** UI components resemble precision diagnostic hardware. Dividers are hairline (1px), margins are strict, and micro-labels guide the eye with monospaced metadata.
- **Layered Obsidian Depths:** Backgrounds use low-luminance contrasts between pitch obsidian (`#050608`), graphite containers (`#101114`), and translucent smoke surfaces to create tactile 3D relief without relying on standard drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette operates in a dark-mode paradigm anchored by pitch black, deep graphite surfaces, and high-energy crimson lighting.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Canvas (`#050608`):** Deep void black. Serves as the canvas floor and background for high-contrast imagery.
- **Surface Elevation (`#101114`):** Secondary container surface. Provides crisp planar separation for cards, navigation panels, and structural blocks.
- **Surface Elevation Hover / Active (`#181A1F`):** Interactive component background for elevated states.
- **Accent Primary / Glow Red (`#FF263B`):** High-voltage indicator used for interactive CTAs, focal gradients, glowing borders, and key system statuses.
- **Accent Secondary / Deep Crimson (`#B51224`):** Deep brand red for rich background gradient meshes, button hover fills, and active glow underlays.
- **Metallic Neutral / Industrial Silver (`#C7CBD2`):** Cool hardware-inspired metallic tone used for structural hairline borders, icons, and micro tags.
- **Text Primary (`#F5F5F7`):** Crisp off-white ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against dark slate surfaces.
- **Text Secondary (`#8E929B`):** Desaturated steel gray for supporting copy, secondary metrics, and unselected states.
- **Hairline Border Rule (`rgba(199, 203, 210, 0.12)`): Ultra-thin boundary definition for precision hardware panels.
- **Crimson Hairline Hover (`rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.35)`): Kinetic border highlight on hover.

### Crimson Light & Gradient Rules
- Gradient text is reserved exclusively for major headlines: a linear 135-degree sweep from `#F5F5F7` through `#FF263B` down to `#B51224`.
- Diffuse volumetric glows must be implemented via radial gradients (`radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.18) 0%, transparent 70%)`) positioned behind hero focal points or critical telemetry cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy communicates both cinematic boldness and industrial precision:
- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Sharp, geometric, with deliberate character quirks that provide an authentic aerospace/robotics agency feel. Set with tight tracking (-0.02em to -0.04em) to deliver an authoritative, dense presence.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Inter):** Highly neutral, readable, and optically balanced. Ensures maximum legibility across dense documentation, project case studies, and lab research reports.
- **Micro-Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** High-density technical annotations, status tags, step indicators (e.g., `// 01 ARCHITECTURE`), coordinate stamps, and sponsor tiers. Monospace labels are uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.12em`).

### Typographic Guidelines
- Section introductions must always pair an uppercase `label-mono-sm` kicker (accented with a leading dot or index indicator) with a tight `headline-xl` title.
- Numbers, metrics, and KPI figures (such as "99.8%", "120+ PARTNERS") must be rendered in `Space Grotesk` with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a precision structural grid reminiscent of advanced diagnostic panels and architectural blueprints.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid system with a maximum content container width of `1360px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `4rem` screen edge margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` canvas margins.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Modular Enclosures:** Elements reside in explicit, hairline-bordered compartmentalized containers rather than floating without boundaries.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Macro-sections maintain deep vertical air using `space-3xl` (`6rem`) margins on desktop, compressing gracefully to `space-2xl` (`4rem`) on mobile.
- **Precision Alignment:** Component headers, labels, and borders snap to exact column boundaries. Corner brackets or HUD crosses (`+`) can be placed at primary layout grid intersections to reinforce the Omni-V industrial aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical illumination, surface refraction, and layered opacity rather than drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Canvas (Base Level 0):** Pure dark background (`#050608`). Anchors primary photography, volumetric crimson lighting, and full-bleed hero visuals.
2. **Structural Panels (Level 1):** Solid `#101114` surfaces with a 1px metallic border (`rgba(199, 203, 210, 0.1)`). Used for modular grid cards, specs blocks, and timeline phases.
3. **Glassmorphic Hud (Level 2):** Translucent backdrop surfaces (`rgba(16, 17, 20, 0.72)`) backed by `16px` background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and a delicate top-edge highlight border (`rgba(245, 245, 247, 0.15)`). Applied to floating navigation bars, modal dialogs, and interactive overlay chips.
4. **Active Crimson Bloom (Level 3):** High-intensity elements project an ambient, soft-diffuse glow using double-box shadows:
   - Subtle State: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -5px rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.35);`
   - Focused / Active State: `box-shadow: 0 0 35px -2px rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.55), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2);`

### Border Accents
- Never use heavy drop shadows on flat panels. Depth is reinforced via subtle metallic hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(199, 203, 210, 0.12)`).
- On card hover, elevate solely through border luminance transition: shifting the border from metallic graphite to vivid crimson (`rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.6)`) alongside a subtle inward radial illumination.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated industrial corner radius balancing modern digital ergonomics with sharp technical hardware framing.

### Corner Radii Hierarchy
- **Base Elements (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard interactive buttons, dropdowns, input fields, badge pills, and inner thumbnail media.
- **Structural Cards (`rounded-lg` / 1.0rem / 16px):** Modular cards, content panels, code preview windows, and feature grid blocks.
- **Hero Containers & Flyouts (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Major showcase windows, modal dialogs, hero media frames, and navigation docks.
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Floating filter tags, status pills (e.g., live badges), and primary rounded-oval action buttons.

### Technical Cut-Outs & Corner Details
- Cards may optionally feature HUD-inspired corner brackets or 45-degree micro-chamfers on specific hero modules to reinforce the lab equipment aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Red:** Background `#FF263B` fading into `#B51224` at a 135-degree angle. Text `#F5F5F7` in `Space Grotesk` medium. Inset top highlight (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). Hover state unleashes a soft red bloom glow (`0 0 24px rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Industrial Metallic:** Ghost surface with `#101114` fill, hairline border (`1px solid rgba(199, 203, 210, 0.2)`), text `#F5F5F7`. Hover turns border to `#FF263B` with text tinting silver-white. Includes trailing directional arrow (`↗`).
- **Tertiary Minimal / Text:** Monospace uppercase font with underline transition and glowing crimson dot indicator.

### Badges & Technical Micro-Chips
- **Status Badges:** Translucent black capsule (`rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.08)`) with hairline crimson border (`rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.3)`). Preceded by a pulsing 6px circular indicator in vivid `#FF263B`.
- **Category Chips:** Rectangular or pill-shaped with `0.5rem` radius, `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase label, and metallic silver border.

### Precision Grid Cards
- **Architecture:** `#101114` graphite container, `1px solid rgba(199, 203, 210, 0.1)` border.
- **Header:** Features an index number (e.g., `[ 01 ]`), component category in secondary text, and an interactive open trigger in the top right corner.
- **Inner Imagery:** High-contrast hardware or schematic visual encased in an inner hairline boundary with a subtle dark vignette.
- **Interactive State:** Hover lifts border color to `rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.5)` with transition duration of `200ms ease-out`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#0A0B0E`, border `1px solid rgba(199, 203, 210, 0.16)`, text `#F5F5F7`, placeholder `#8E929B`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#FF263B` with a companion subtle box shadow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 38, 59, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp or rounded-sm metal frame. Active checked state fills with crimson red and displays an off-white vector tick or radio pip.

### Sponsor & Partner Showcase Modules
- Grid-locked enterprise logo tier. Monochromatic silver SVGs rendered at 60% opacity against the `#050608` canvas, lighting up to 100% pure metallic `#F5F5F7` with a low crimson aura upon hover.
- Structural hairline grid lines divide each sponsor cell continuously across 4 or 6 columns.

### Code & Engineering Telemetry Blocks
- Darkened `#08090C` background, top HUD bar with simulated system status (`SYS.OK // 60 FPS`), line-numbered monospace code views, and accent syntax highlighting dominated by crimson red, silver, and muted gray.
